Why I chose sports as my theme

From a very young age, I've always had a huge interest in sports. It started off with me just being an energetic child, so I was always looking for ways to stay active. My father and older brother were also a huge reason why I love sports, and we would often bond by watching a good game of football. I watch sports, play sports, manage fantasy sports teams, and keep up with sports analytics. Sports was a no-brainer for me when picking a theme since it's one of my favourite pastimes.
